Piece icon library

115 drawings in 216 files. Each is drawn as the board draws it: the top row in the light theme, the bottom row in the dark one, each row the first player's piece on the light and the dark square and then the second player's. Shogi tiles are one file, the second player's turned round. The western pieces are one set in Cburnett's grammar; shogi and xiangqi are their own families. Licensing per file is in CREDITS.md; how the new drawings are made is in draw.py, and check.py sorts them by silhouette at 24 px.
